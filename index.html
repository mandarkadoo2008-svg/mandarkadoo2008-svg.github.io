Four films I can’t shake. Each one comes with a teaser (no spoilers) and three more films to watch next.

Fight Club

1999 · Directed by David Fincher

An insomniac office worker, numb from a life of flat-pack furniture and fluorescent lights, meets a charming stranger who offers a very different way to feel alive. What starts as a secret meeting in a basement slowly grows into something neither of them can control.

Watch next

  • American Psycho (2000)Another sharp, dark look at a man hiding behind a polished surface.
  • Taxi Driver (1976)A lonely night-shift driver and a city that wears him down.
  • Trainspotting (1996)Rebellious, funny and reckless, with the same anger at “normal” life.

Into the Wild

2007 · Directed by Sean Penn

A bright young graduate gives away his savings, drops his old name and hitchhikes north, chasing a life with no rules and no one to answer to. Every person he meets along the way leaves a mark, and the road keeps pulling him toward Alaska.

Watch next

  • Wild (2014)A woman walks a thousand miles of trail to put her life back together.
  • 127 Hours (2010)A solo hiker, a remote canyon and a test of will.
  • The Way (2010)A father walks a pilgrimage in his son’s place and finds more than he expected.

Kill Bill

2003 · Directed by Quentin Tarantino

Left for dead on her wedding day, a former assassin wakes up years later with a short list of names and one goal. Her path to the end of that list runs through swordfights, old teachers and the people she once called family.

Watch next

  • Oldboy (2003)A man held captive for years has days to find out why.
  • Lady Snowblood (1973)The classic revenge story that inspired Tarantino’s style.
  • John Wick (2014)Stylish, fast and built entirely around one personal loss.

Se7en

1995 · Directed by David Fincher

A weary detective one week from retirement is paired with an eager newcomer, and together they chase a killer whose crimes follow a disturbing pattern. The rain never stops, and the deeper they dig, the less they like what they find.

Watch next

  • Zodiac (2007)A real-life hunt for a killer that slowly takes over everyone’s life.
  • Memories of Murder (2003)Korean detectives, a rural town and a case that won’t close.
  • Prisoners (2013)A father takes matters into his own hands when the police run out of leads.
